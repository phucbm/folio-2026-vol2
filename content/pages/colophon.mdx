## Technology

This site is a [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) 16 app exported as static files and served from [Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/static-assets/) static assets. There is no server: every page is HTML built ahead of time. Notes and archived projects are MDX files in the repo.

## Type

- **Source Serif 4** for the name and section titles.
- **Source Sans 3** for reading text, with old-style figures.
- **IBM Plex Mono** for numbers, dates and IDs.

All three are self-hosted and cut down to the characters the site uses (latin and Vietnamese).
