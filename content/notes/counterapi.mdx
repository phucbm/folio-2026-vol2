I wanted two numbers for [Hiểu Chữ Hán](https://github.com/phucbm/hieu-chu-han): how many times it is opened and how many people installed it as an app. GA4, PostHog and Umami all do far more than that and ask for a dashboard or a consent banner. [counterapi.dev](https://counterapi.dev) is a counter behind a URL: call `/up` to add one, read it back as `{ count }`. The app shows both numbers in a small badge.

## The rules

- **Never count in development.** Every reload while I work would be a visit, so the increment only runs in production.
- **Never throw.** The counter is a nice-to-have. Every call is wrapped and fails silently; reads fall back to zero.
- **Show nothing rather than a zero.** The badge renders only once a number arrives, so a slow or failed call leaves no "0 visits".

What it can't do: no referrers, no pages, nothing per day.
